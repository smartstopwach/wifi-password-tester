📶 WiFi QR — Password Nikalo

Saved WiFi ka password QR se nikalo — bina root, bina install, bina disconnect

STEP 0  |  PEHLE QR KAHA SE LANA HAI

  1. Settings kholo → Wi-Fi par jao
  2. Jis network ka password chahiye, uske naam par tap karo
  3. Share / QR code option dabao → ek QR dikhega
  4. Wahi QR yahan scan karo, ya uske neeche likha text copy karke paste karo

💡 Ye sirf unhi WiFi ka password dikha sakta hai jo aapke phone me pehle se save hain (jo aap kabhi connect kar chuke ho).

STEP 1  |  TARIKA CHUNO

Camera sirf Chrome me kaam karta hai (ye page phone ke browser me kholna). Nahi chale to "Text Paste" use karo.
